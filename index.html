01. About

I'm an undergraduate in Statistics & Machine Learning at Carnegie Mellon University. Outside of class I work as a GTM Engineer at Novig, a peer-to-peer sports prediction market, where I build internal tools, data pipelines, and the automation that helps the go-to-market team move faster.

My side projects live at the intersection of probability and software: I've built and operated an automated market-making bot for prediction markets, which taught me more about measurement, honesty with data, and production systems than any single course.

This site is my running portfolio for CMU's 15-113 (Effective Coding with AI) and beyond. Each new project gets added here as the semester goes on.

bid · ask

Prediction-Market Making Bot

An automated market maker for sports contracts on Kalshi. It aggregates prices from multiple sportsbooks into a consensus fair value, rests post-only quotes around it, and manages inventory and risk limits. Runs 24/7 on AWS with paper-trading and shadow modes for testing changes before they touch real money.
