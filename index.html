Quantitative Trading & Market-Making Developer · New York, NY · May 2026 – Present

  • Built a first-principles same-game parlay pricing engine from scratch: a Gaussian-copula joint-probability model matching a paid vendor's correlation-adjusted prices to within ±1% across 1,000+ held-out games in four leagues (MLB, NBA, NHL, WNBA); generalized the 2-leg analytic form to an N-leg multivariate copula (nearest-PSD repair, Cholesky factorization, seeded Monte Carlo) and isolated a durable ∼3–4 point edge vs. soft-book lines through adversarial walk-forward backtests
