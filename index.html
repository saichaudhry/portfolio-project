Novig — Pricing & Market Making

Work on the pricing, quoting, and market-making stack of a peer-to-peer prediction-market exchange. Built a first-principles same-game parlay pricing engine using a Gaussian-copula joint-probability model, generalized from two legs to N legs, and validated it against a paid vendor with walk-forward backtests. Worked on cross-venue market matching across Kalshi, Novig, and Polymarket, on the framework that lets external market makers stream two-sided liquidity into the exchange, and on restoring the live parlay request-for-quote funnel.

bid · ask

MAG — Trading Systems

Trading-systems work for MAG, a market-making firm active on prediction markets. Built an automated market maker for Kalshi sports contracts: a recorder for exchange order books and sportsbook odds, a de-vig model that turns them into a consensus fair price, a passive quoting engine with inventory and exposure limits, and a replay backtester that re-runs recorded days against real settlements before changes go live. Deployed on AWS with paper and shadow modes and a scorecard built around closing-line value.

  • Python
  • AWS EC2
  • REST + WebSockets
  • SQLite
  • Statistics
